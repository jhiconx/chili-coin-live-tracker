# CHI × Cronos — The Everyday Onramp

Three-page, responsive static PWA: (1) high-level partnership concept, (2) distribution, and (3) editable growth model with CSV export. All scenario outputs are hypothetical, not committed or verified operational scale.

## Key baseline math

15,000 stores × 100 modeled unique shoppers/store/year = 1,500,000 unique grocery shoppers. With 50% participating, 10 CHI per earned reward and 52 eligible rewards/year, grocery distribution is 390,000,000 CHI/year, 32,500,000/month, and ~1,068,493/day.

The model adds radio, campus and film cohorts, applies an overlap reduction to added cohorts, and only then computes unique wallet onboarding. It never pays the same person twice simply for repeated activity. Wallet CPA of $100 is only a proposed commercial assumption.

## Important constraints

- CHI is an existing Ethereum/Base asset. Cronos distribution, exchange listing and CHI/CRO trading are proposals, not live features.
- Crypto.com wallet enrollment, fiat features and transfers require compatible networks, eligibility, KYC where applicable, and a separately negotiated integration.
- 15 radio stations, 150 colleges, 15,000 stores, H-E-B partnership, new film studio rights and $100 fee are planning proposals unless independently confirmed.
- Stock photography is illustrative; product imagery/logos are from the public GFT/CHI repository and public Cronos/Crypto.com resources.
- No backend credentials; no collection of personal data; model runs client-side and CSV exports locally.

## Files

`index.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`. Deploy as a zero-build static site. The PWA shell works offline once visited; third-party images/fonts may be missing offline.
