The thesis

Don't ask people to buy crypto.
Give them a reason to use it.

Every purchase, broadcast, brand sampling event and entertainment offer can become a verified opportunity to earn CHI—and, subject to a commercial agreement, unlock CRO.

①

Meet them where they are

At the register, in a radio promotion, on campus or through a movie offer. No trading app required to begin the CHI rewards experience.

②

Reward a real action

Verify a purchase, redemption or approved engagement. Deliver CHI into a phone-linked shopper wallet. Sponsor-funded CRO is the proposed next unlock.

③

Open the wider world

Eligible customers can opt into Crypto.com and potentially deposit, hold, trade or use CRO, subject to account eligibility and supported networks.

A 15,000-store network × 100 unique shoppers per store annually gives 1.5M unique shoppers; 50% participation, 10 CHI per qualifying reward, and 52 rewards per user per year gives 390M CHI per year from grocery. All are user-defined assumptions.

Important distinction: a wallet is not a Crypto.com customer.
The wallet-count output is only a modeled qualified-wallet opportunity. A payable acquisition event would need a mutually agreed definition, proof of uniqueness and newness, KYC/account requirements where relevant, consent, partner validation, attribution window and anti-fraud protections. A $100 fee and CHI/CRO trading pair are unexecuted proposals.

What happens to the CHI supply?

These are distributions of existing CHI, not new issuance. The October 2026 whitepaper reported 100B total CHI supply and 20B earmarked for a GFT reward allocation; the allocation does not prove those tokens are bridged or available on Base.

Can CRO be moved to Crypto.com?

Crypto.com documents deposit support for CRO on specified networks. Transfers require an eligible account, compatible network and wallet/address details. Trading and fiat conversions depend on region, verification and product availability.

Are new wallets automatically unique?

No. The model counts each participating person once for potential qualified onboarding. It does not bill for repeat earning events or every technical wallet object created.

Would the token growth be exponential?

The model grows with its input assumptions. Repeat engagement, referrals and multiple channels could compound adoption, but exponential growth is not established or guaranteed by this forecast.
