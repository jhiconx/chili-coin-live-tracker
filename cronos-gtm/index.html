Are 12 shopping trips 12 unique shoppers?

No. It's one person shopping 12 times in a month. The visits may qualify for recurring promotions without opening new wallets.

Do repeat visits add more unique shoppers?

No. The unique shopper base is calculated once. Changing monthly grocery visits changes repeat transaction volume, not the unique shopper count.

Can each visit earn rewards?

Only when a qualifying promotion applies. Eligibility and the annual reward-frequency limit control modeled token transfers.

Does every reward create a new wallet?

No. A wallet pair is created only for an eligible first-time shopper. Further rewards reuse that pair.
